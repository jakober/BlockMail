# BlockMail — Play Store listing (English, en-US)

Paste these into Play Console → Store listing → Manage translations → English (United States).

## App name (max 30 characters)

BlockMail – Smart Email App

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Focus blocks, phishing guard, reply radar: the mail app that thinks ahead

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

BlockMail is the clear, fast email app that thinks ahead: your inbox sorts
itself by importance, a phishing guard warns you about scams, and the reply
radar makes sure nothing important slips through — with no ads and no
tracking.

**Focus blocks — unique to BlockMail**
One tap and your inbox groups itself into clear blocks: "Needs a reply",
"Important", "Can wait" and "Ads & newsletters". Instead of one endless
list, you simply work through the blocks in the morning. On request, AI
refines the sorting — right on your device.

**Phishing guard — security without the cloud**
BlockMail checks every email for typical scam patterns: links that lead
somewhere other than displayed, spoofed senders of well-known brands,
disguised domains and pressure wording. Suspicious emails get a red warning
banner with an explanation. The check runs entirely on your device — no
data ever leaves your phone.

**Reply radar — nothing slips through**
The app detects emails with open questions you haven't answered yet and
reminds you once a day. And the other way round: if you've been waiting
days for a reply, BlockMail suggests following up.

**Ready in one minute**
Pick your provider, enter your email address and password — done. The
setup assistant knows the servers of Gmail, Web.de, GMX, T-Online,
Outlook/Hotmail, Yahoo and iCloud and configures everything automatically.
Custom IMAP servers can be set up manually as well.

**All accounts, one overview**
Connect multiple mailboxes and switch in a flash — or see everything
together in the unified "All accounts" inbox. Give each account its own
color: the colored bar on every email instantly shows where it belongs.

**Real-time notifications**
BlockMail keeps a direct connection to your mailbox and reports new mail
instantly — with sender pictures like a messenger. Reply straight from the
notification without opening the app. Senders can be muted or blocked
entirely.

**Newsletters? Tidied up.**
The built-in newsletter detection automatically sorts promotional mail into
its own folder and finds the matching unsubscribe link. Your inbox stays
free for the things that matter.

**AI that runs on your device**
Long email? One tap gives you a short summary. Let the AI draft replies,
phrase messages or fix your spelling. By default the AI runs directly on
your device (Gemini Nano) — your emails never leave your phone for it.
Optionally you can add your own Claude API key.

**A joy to use**
• Unique tile view: emails as blocks — or a classic list if you prefer
• Customizable swipe gestures: delete, archive, mark read, remind
• "Undo" after deleting — nothing is ever gone by accident
• Reply all, drafts with auto-save, templates and signatures
• Snooze and scheduled send at your preferred time
• Full-text search on the server — finds emails from years ago
• Attachment gallery: every attachment from every email in one place
• Statistics in tile style: top senders, volume, attachment ratio
• Conversation view, time groups and preview text in the overview
• Home screen widget and app shortcuts for quick access
• Light and dark theme, color schemes and a free color picker
• Backup & move: export your settings as a file and take them along

**Your data is yours**
No BlockMail servers, no ads, no analytics: credentials are stored
encrypted on your device, and your email travels directly between you and
your provider. Preloaded content is cleaned up automatically after a week.

Note: Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud require an app password from your provider —
the setup assistant guides you there step by step with a direct link.
